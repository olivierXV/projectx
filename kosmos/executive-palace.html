The President

Zarpaii Xarpeius

Zarpaii Xarpeius is the President of the Federal Republic of Latrevia. She was elected in 164 AAX, in a landslide, at the age of 78, in the first presidential election held under the Constitution of the Third Republic.

She is the daughter of Xarpeius, the Deliverer, and of King Cassius of Solaria, and the younger sister of Latreus. When her brother renounced the Solarian throne in 102 AAX she became Queen of Solaria at sixteen, and she has reigned in her own right since 107 AAX. She is the only person to have held both crowns at once, an arrangement the Commonwealth Charter permits for this officeholder alone (Art. 31).

She trained in law and served as a federal judge until she resigned from the bench in 137 AAX, as the executive under her brother tightened its hold on the Republic. She spent the following years building the coalition that would restore it.

On 1 Ixarius 156 AAX she declared the Third Republic Coalition. After the Duaterre Crossing in 159 AAX she chose not to storm Xareionople. She offered amnesty to soldiers who laid down their arms and safe passage to those who wished to leave, and on 12 Ennia 160 AAX the city surrendered without a shot. She then chaired the Interim Council through the Constitutional Convention and the adoption of the Commonwealth Charter.

Her government has concentrated on bedding in the Control Commission and the Civil Service Commission, standing up the Commonwealth’s institutions, and the Lancian Recovery Act.

Prime Minister and Cabinet · Art. 11–12

Who governs day to day

The President appoints the Prime Minister from the leader of the majority coalition in the House. Ministers are appointed on the Prime Minister’s advice and confirmed by the House. Members of the Assembly give up their seats on appointment (Art. 13).

AL
Prime Minister · Head of government

Astrid de Lune

Centre Democratic Party, leader of the Third Republic Coalition

Leads the Cabinet, coordinates the federal ministries and sets the government’s legislative programme. She answers questions in the House at least once a fortnight, and can be removed only by a constructive vote of no confidence that names her successor (Art. 11).

The House and the governing coalition

The Third Republic Coalition is the wartime alliance carried into government: the Centre Democratic Party, the Social Progressive Party, the Labour and Land Party and the Eastern Alliance. The United Conservatives lead the opposition. Twenty percent of House seats are held by sectoral representatives, most of whom vote with the government.

Article 10

What the President can and cannot do

The Presidency was redesigned after the Second Republic, when too much power sat in one office and too few checks sat around it.

Powers

  • Represents the Federation internationallyArt. 10
  • Signs or returns bills within fifteen days. A veto falls to two thirds of both chambersArt. 10, 15
  • Appoints the Prime Minister from the majority coalitionArt. 10, 11
  • Appoints and dismisses ministers on the Prime Minister’s advice, subject to House confirmationArt. 10, 12
  • Commander-in-chief of the armed forcesArt. 10
  • May declare a national emergency, but only within Title VArt. 31

Limits

  • Two terms of five years in total, consecutive or notArt. 10, 54
  • Elected directly, in two roundsArt. 10, 54
  • May not dissolve the Assembly, except after a failed constructive vote of no confidence and 30 days without a new Prime MinisterArt. 10
  • May be investigated by the Control CommissionArt. 26
  • Impeached by two thirds of the House, removed by two thirds of the Senate under the Chief JusticeArt. 26
  • No relative within the second degree may follow into the same officeArt. 54
No national emergency is in force.

An emergency may be declared only for invasion or war, a national disaster or epidemic, or a state’s breakdown of constitutional order. It goes to the Assembly within 24 hours and lapses after 30 days unless renewed (Art. 31).

Presidential oath · Appendix I

I, Zarpaii Xarpeius, having been elected President of the Federal Republic of Latrevia, do solemnly swear before the People of Latrevia and before history:

That I will faithfully execute the office of President; that I will to the best of my ability preserve, protect, and defend the Constitution of the Third Republic; that I will never use the power of this office to subvert the democratic order, to silence dissent, or to serve any master other than the People and the law; that I remember the Theocracy and will never be its servant; that I remember the Empire and will never be its heir.

Commonwealth Charter · Art. 10

President of the Commonwealth by virtue of office

The President of Latrevia is the President of the Commonwealth of Latrevia and Solaria. There is no separate Commonwealth presidential election, and the Commonwealth term is the same five years as the Latrevian one.

In that capacity the President represents the Commonwealth in diplomacy, signs Commonwealth laws with the power to return them, commands the Commonwealth Defence Force, ratifies treaties the Congress has approved, and proclaims Commonwealth emergencies. Those acts answer to the Commonwealth Congress and the Commonwealth Constitutional Court, not only to Latrevian review.

The Office of the Commonwealth President sits at this Palace in Xareionople. The Commonwealth Congress and the Prime Minister sit at the Dome of the Commonwealth in Latreionople.
